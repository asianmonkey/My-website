Studio Handajuwana Zhu is an interior and architecture practice which works across all scales and disciplines, from residential and public buildings to interiors and furniture. Established in 2026 based in Jakarta, Indonesia with backgrounds in Japan and the Netherlands, we bring fresh perspectives shaped by international experiences. Each project responds to the people and place, grounded in cultural, economic, and environmental context. Designing with simplicity, space, atmosphere, and clarity, we create buildings and designs that last socially and physically. We work through all stages of the process from concept to completion, tailored towards the project.
